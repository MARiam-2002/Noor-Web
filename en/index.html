THE IDEA BEHIND NOOR

Less noise. More meaning.

Noor is a modern Islamic app built around a simple idea: meaningful worship can be part of everyday life. One considered space for the Quran, prayer, remembrance, and your personal journey.

ABOUT THE APP

Questions about Noor

What is Noor?

Noor is an Islamic app that brings together Quran reading, prayer times, adhkar, tasbih, Qibla, and reading progress.

Can I download Noor now?

Yes. Noor is available now on Google Play for Android, and the iPhone version is coming soon.

Does the app support Arabic?

Yes. Noor is available in Arabic and English, and you can switch the language inside the app anytime.
